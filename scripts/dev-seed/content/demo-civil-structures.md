# Question 1

In a simply supported beam carrying a single central point load, where is the bending moment largest?

- A. At the supports
- B. At the centre of the span
- C. At a quarter of the span
- D. It is the same everywhere

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

For a central point load, the bending moment rises linearly from zero at each support to its maximum, WL/4, under the load at mid-span.

**Topic:** Bending Moment

# Question 2

A steel rod stretches by 1 mm under a load. If the same load is applied to a rod of the same material and length but twice the cross-sectional area, how much does it stretch?

- A. 0.25 mm
- B. 0.5 mm
- C. 1 mm
- D. 2 mm

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

Extension is PL/AE. Doubling the area A halves the extension, from 1 mm to 0.5 mm.

**Topic:** Stress and Strain

# Question 3

Which support type prevents translation in both directions but allows rotation?

- A. Roller support
- B. Pinned support
- C. Fixed support
- D. Free end

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

A pinned (hinged) support provides horizontal and vertical reactions but no moment, so the member can rotate about it. A fixed support also resists rotation; a roller resists movement in one direction only.

**Topic:** Supports and Reactions

# Question 4

The ratio of lateral strain to longitudinal strain in a loaded bar is called

- A. Young's modulus
- B. Poisson's ratio
- C. Bulk modulus
- D. Strain energy

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

Poisson's ratio is the (negative) ratio of lateral to longitudinal strain; for most metals it is around 0.25 to 0.33.

**Topic:** Stress and Strain
