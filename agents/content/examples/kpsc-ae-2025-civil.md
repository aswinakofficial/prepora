# Question 1

What is the unit of modulus of elasticity?

- A. N
- B. N/mm²
- C. mm/N
- D. N/mm

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

Modulus of elasticity E = Stress / Strain. Stress has units N/mm² and strain is
dimensionless, so E is measured in N/mm².

**Topic:** Strength of Materials

# Question 2

The ratio of lateral strain to linear strain is called:

- A. Poisson's ratio
- B. Young's modulus
- C. Bulk modulus
- D. Modulus of rigidity

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**

Poisson's ratio (ν) = Lateral strain / Longitudinal strain. It is a dimensionless
constant typically between 0 and 0.5 for most materials.

**Topic:** Strength of Materials

# Question 3

A simply supported beam of span L carries a uniformly distributed load of
intensity w per unit length. The maximum bending moment is:

- A. wL²/4
- B. wL²/8
- C. wL/2
- D. wL²/12

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:**

For a simply supported beam with UDL, the maximum bending moment occurs at
mid-span and equals wL²/8.

**Topic:** Theory of Structures

# Question 4

Which of the following statements about concrete is correct?

- A. Concrete is strong in tension
- B. Concrete is weak in compression
- C. Concrete is strong in compression but weak in tension
- D. Concrete has equal strength in tension and compression

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:**

Concrete has high compressive strength (typically 20–40 MPa) but very low tensile
strength (about 1/10 of its compressive strength). This is why steel reinforcement
is used in tension zones.

**Topic:** Concrete Technology
