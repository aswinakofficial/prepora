# Code of Conduct

Prepora is built by students, teachers and developers from many places and backgrounds. We want
everyone who contributes, asks a question or reports a problem to feel welcome and respected.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to open source
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what's best for the community and for learners

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all community spaces — issues, pull requests, Discussions, code review, and any
other channel run by the project — and when someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it **privately** to the
maintainer, [@aswinakofficial](https://github.com/aswinakofficial), using the contact details on
that GitHub profile — not in a public issue or discussion. All reports are reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, depending on the impact and intent:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning with clarity about what was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a period of no
   interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards individuals
   or groups.
