# Other certification vendors

> **Verdict:** Mostly **topic structure only**. Their official practice material is too small,
> behind forms or paywalls, has no answers, or forbids copying.
> **Researched:** 2026-10-01. Unconfirmed details are marked UNVERIFIED.

For certifications, Prepora uses only **vendor-published practice material**. Real exam questions
are under candidate NDAs, and dump sites are never used ([not-onboarded](not-onboarded.md)).
These vendors were researched alongside [ISTQB](istqb.md), [AWS](aws.md),
[HashiCorp](hashicorp.md) and [Databricks](databricks.md).

Even where questions can't be used, a vendor's **exam outline** (domains, objectives) can create
the exam and its topic structure. Prepora can then link out to the vendor's own free practice page.
